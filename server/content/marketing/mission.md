## AI training and shared outcomes

The **Neon Law Foundation** advances AI training and the outcomes people produce together. The work may happen through
the firm or through the nonprofit; its purpose is to help people learn, practice, and improve what they create together.

## Learn with judgment

We teach people to use AI with **judgment, collaboration, and adversarial review**. Training makes room for careful
questions, clear reasoning, and the human responsibility to decide what belongs in the work.

## Work together

Education is a practice people build with one another. We make space for people to compare approaches, explain their
thinking, and learn from the outcomes their collaboration produces.

## Review before relying

Adversarial review is part of the learning. Participants test assumptions, look for what a first answer missed, and
invite a different perspective before relying on an outcome.
